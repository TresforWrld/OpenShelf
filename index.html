    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: July 20, 2026

    Overview

    OpenShelf is a free book discovery and reading tool. This policy explains what information the app touches and how it's handled. OpenShelf does not have user accounts, does not run its own backend server, and does not sell or share personal data.

    Information Stored On Your Device

    OpenShelf saves the following data directly in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never transmitted to OpenShelf or ANICADE Tech:

    • Books you've saved to your favourites/library
    • Your reading progress and page position for each book
    • Personal annotations and notes you add while reading
    • Display preferences, such as font size and dark mode
    • Your recent search history, used only to power search suggestions

    Clearing your browser data, or using the "Clear All" option in your Library, permanently removes this information.

    Third-Party Services

    To find and display books, OpenShelf sends your search terms and requests to the following external, independently-operated services:

    • Open Library (openlibrary.org) — book search, metadata, and cover images
    • Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org) — full public-domain book text
    • Gutendex (gutendex.com) — a catalog API for Project Gutenberg titles

    These requests are subject to the respective privacy practices of those services, which OpenShelf does not control. OpenShelf recommends reviewing their policies if you have concerns.

    What OpenShelf Does Not Do

    • No account creation, login, or password collection
    • No advertising trackers or ad networks
    • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties
    • No server-side storage of your reading activity

    Text-to-Speech

    The "Read Aloud" feature uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis. Book text is processed locally by your browser/operating system and is not sent to OpenShelf's or any third party's servers for this purpose.

    Children's Privacy

    OpenShelf does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since no personal data is collected at all beyond what's described above.

    Changes to This Policy

    This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the app. Continued use of OpenShelf after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

    OpenShelf is built and maintained by ANICADE Tech. Questions can be sent to anicadetech@gmail.com.
